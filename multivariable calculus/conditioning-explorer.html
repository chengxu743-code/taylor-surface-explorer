Why the ratio matters

The steep side sets the scale of a safe step; here we choose 1/L. The flat side offers a gradient of only m times the distance. Each step removes m/L of the flat-direction distance, so a small ratio takes many steps.

Try “Taller, same ratio”

Both curvatures double: the cross-section curves rise and the fixed-height contours shrink. But m/L stays 0.1, so gradient descent makes the same relative progress when its step size is adjusted to 1/L. This is expected.
